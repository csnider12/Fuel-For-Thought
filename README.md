# Fuel-For-Thought
2024 Ohio Academy of Science Project Submission

The purpose of our experiment was so we could determine what the differences between various fuels were in their performance. The question we asked was, what type of gasoline/ fuel is the most efficient when considering the value of watts/ second and kilojoules of energy produced per 70 mL of gasoline/ fuel? We hypothesized that eighty-eight octane will perform slightly worse than the 10% ethanol fuels and E85 to perform the worst due to the decreased energy density of ethanol. We believe that ninety-octane will perform slightly better than any of the other fuel types due to it having the highest energy density. We conducted the experiment by adding 70mL of the selected fuel into the generator. We then started the generator, space heater, arduino, and computer code. Next, we organized the data from the arduino into a spreadsheet and repeated for each trial. These tests led us to believe that ninety octane is the most efficient out of the fuels we tested. It also showed that the 10% ethanol fuels were very similar to each other. Also as expected, the eighty-eight octane and E85 were the worst performers. This led us to conclude that there is really no point to getting a more premium, and expensive fuel if your only concern is efficiency.
